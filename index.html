Questions students ask

Is this just ChatGPT with a nicer skin?

No. StudyForgeAI keeps a model of your exam — topics, accuracy per topic, readiness, and a day-by-day plan. The AI writes the questions and explanations, but the planning and weak-topic detection are the product. That's what makes tomorrow's session different from today's.

What can I upload?

Typed notes, pasted text, PDFs, PowerPoints, worksheets and photos of handwritten pages. Text-based material gets turned into questions and flashcards automatically; images and slides are indexed to the topic they belong to.

How much time do I need each day?

Whatever you honestly have. Tell it 20 minutes and it builds 20-minute sessions. Consistency beats cramming — the plan assumes you'll actually do it.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes, anytime in Account → Subscription. You drop back to Free and keep all your exams and history.
